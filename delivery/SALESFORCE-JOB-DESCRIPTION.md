Overview

Do you want to be part of an outstanding team selling into the world's top organizations? Come join the Salesforce Solution Engineering team! Through collaboration with the customer, the Sales team, the Product team, and Professional Services, our Solution Engineers (SEs) drive the technical sale of our products and help customers realize real business value. This unique perspective creates an opportunity for the SE to interact with the entire customer lifecycle. If you enjoy a fast-paced environment full of exciting challenges and opportunities to build compelling solutions, this Solution Engineering role may be right for you. T
he SE world is a dynamic, constantly evolving environment where expertise in design, business consulting, and technology is used every day to drive innovation.

As a Solution Engineer at Salesforce, you must have a strong desire to demonstrate technical and sales skills, including the ability to drive discovery, solicit business requirements, develop a technical sales strategy, and configure and successfully demonstrate the solutions that address those requirements and deliver business value. We hire based on skills and experience and are open to hiring at all levels for the right candidate.


What Role Are We Hiring For?

The Salesforce Solutions team is organized into account-focused/industry SEs and product specialist SEs. This role is for an
Analytics Specialist Solution Engineer (Tableau) supporting our Consumer Business Services (CBS) vertical
. The ideal candidate has a passion for helping customers solve complex business problems with analytics and AI. Experience with Salesforce, AI, Tableau and/or CRM Analytics/Tableau Next is required. You'll bring creativity to incorporating analytics concepts into comprehensive visions that span the entire Salesforce platform, lead from the front by showing customers how to bring agentic, data-driven experiences to their end users today, and partner with our Account and Analytics sales teams to showcase the right technical solution to drive value and efficiency for our customers.


Responsibilities

Play a pivotal role in aligning innovative solutions to technology challenges within complex CBS accounts, partnering with clients and collaborating with internal team members to drive consensus on multi-product technology solutions across Salesforce's product lines, with a primary focus on the Analytics portfolio

Handle business development, operations strategy, and product demonstrations as a trusted advisor for innovative solutions

Business Problem Solving:
 Define ambiguous business problems through research and assessment, drive innovative ideas, and recommend practical next steps

Innovation Management:
 Focus client decision-making by evaluating solutions and driving stakeholder consensus; help build decision frameworks that deliver ongoing value

Connect Experiences:
 Demonstrate connected, data-driven experiences through software demos and rapid prototyping with Salesforce and Tableau

Build customized solutions and demonstrations for multiple personas, showing how platforms accelerate strategy and deliver value

Understand and advise on customer data and analytics architecture as it relates to Salesforce and Tableau solutions

Participate in all appropriate product, sales, and procedural training and certifications to maintain the knowledge needed to be effective in the role

Solid oral, written, presentation, collaboration, and interpersonal communication skills
