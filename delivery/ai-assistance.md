## Human-control model

The implementation followed a controlled sequence:

1. Dakshit set the objective and non-negotiable boundaries.
2. Requirements and acceptance criteria were written before the finished UI.
3. The source and architecture were kept bounded.
4. The real data was allowed to fail the first quality gate.
5. Failures were investigated and documented.
6. Tests and independent runtime checks were used to verify AI-produced work.
7. Public actions were withheld for explicit approval.
