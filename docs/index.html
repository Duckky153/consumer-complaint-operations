Before acting

Internal evidence required

The public signal generates questions; internal data determines action.

Validate the hypothesis

Request transaction and account denominators, process step and channel, repeat contacts, reopens, resolution time, consumer feedback, and policy-exception evidence.

Decision guardrail

Do not interpret complaint counts as defect rates or company rankings, or use them alone for staffing or policy changes. The dataset lacks customer volume, market share, complaint propensity, case inventory, and controllability evidence.

Methodology

Method and limits

What is included

The complete published CFPB population for the selected product and period. Metrics are produced in SQLite and reconciled to the same minimized, encoded record-level rows used by the filters.

What is excluded

Consumer narratives, ZIP codes, complaint IDs, and company public response prose never reach the public dashboard. State, tags, and submission channel are not ingested. No individual complaint is displayed.

What cannot be concluded

CFPB complaints are not a statistical sample of consumer harm. Counts do not control for company size, geography, product usage, or differences in the likelihood of filing a complaint. Issue labels are intake categories, not validated process causes.
